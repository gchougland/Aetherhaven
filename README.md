# Aetherhaven

**A Hytale mod by Hexvane**

Aetherhaven is a town building mod for Hytale. Found a settlement in the world, raise buildings for villagers, grow your economy, and turn a quiet patch of land into a bustling community. The mod was created for the Hytale New Worlds Modding Contest 2026 (NPCs / Experiences).

Download the latest release on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/aetherhaven).

---

## What you do

You start with a **town charter** and a **planning desk**. Place the charter to claim your town borders, then follow quests from **Lyren the Elder** to build your first structures. From there you expand with farms, shops, production buildings, parks, walls, and more. Villagers move in, work their jobs, pay taxes, and ask for your help through story quests and daily jobs.

Most of what you need to learn is explained inside the game through the **Town Journal**, which includes a full guide, quest tracking, town management, and settings.

## Features

### Founding and growing a town

- Place a **town charter** to define your town borders and name your settlement
- Invite friends to join your town and share building progress
- Choose **town difficulty** when founding (Easy, Normal, Hard, or custom building costs)
- See your town borders on the world map with a waypoint you can toggle in the journal
- Move or dissolve your town through the charter when you need a fresh start

### Building

- Stake building plots with the **plot placement staff**, then bring materials to each **plot sign** to finish construction
- Use the **building staff** to paint structures into place, or let buildings assemble passively over time
- Craft **plot tokens** at the **plot crafting bench**, which shows a 3D preview of each building
- Unlock some buildings by finding **plot blueprints** in floating gifts
- Upgrade production buildings through the **town records shelf**
- Lay out **stone walls** with the wall wand in a top-down view
- Build **paths** through town with the path tool
- Use **wood scaffolding** to reach higher places while building
- **Move completed buildings** to a new spot without starting over

### Villagers and life in town

- Meet a cast of hand-crafted villagers: the Elder, Innkeeper, Merchant, Blacksmith, Priestess, Farmer, Rancher, Miner, Logger, and more
- **Townsfolk** with unique personalities fill out the town as guards, tourists, and background residents
- Assign villagers to **workplaces** and optional **houses** so they can sleep, work, and stay comfortable
- Keep villagers happy by managing **hunger**, **energy**, and **fun** (parks and benches help with fun)
- **Gift items** to villagers to raise **reputation** and unlock recipes, tools, and special rewards
- Villagers follow **daily schedules**, commute to work, and react to the world around them

### Quests

- **Story quests** from villagers guide you through founding the town and unlocking new residents and buildings
- **Daily quests** include fetch jobs, hunting tasks, and **raid quests** where mobs march toward town
- **Guild hall quest boards** offer tiered fetch jobs with rewards and time limits
- **Inn visitors** bring their own quests and can sometimes be invited to stay permanently

### Economy and production

- Earn **gold coins** from a morning **tithe** based on how well your residents are doing
- Store and manage gold in the **treasury** at the town hall
- Host **feasts** at the banquet table for bonuses and town events
- Production buildings (farm, mine, lumber mill, barn, and others) gather resources over time
- Break world containers for a chance to find loose gold coins while exploring

### Shops and trade

- NPC shops restock at dawn and sell through **shop spots** in each building
- Build a **player shop** and list items for sale; villagers and tourists will browse and buy
- Open a **tourist portal** to welcome visitors who walk around town, shop, and can be invited to live with you
- Visit Vex's general store, the Florist, Pyrotechnic, Crystal Keeper, Builder, and other specialty shops as you unlock them

### Combat and defense

- Hire **adventurers** from the **guild hall** as **guards** who patrol and fight hostile mobs
- Draw patrol routes with the **patrol wand**
- Use the **command post** for a top-down RTS view where you can direct groups of guards
- **Purification powder** reveals and cleanses nearby enemy spawn points before they threaten your town

### Exploration and extras

- **Floating gifts** drift by on balloons. Shoot them down for gold, furniture, decoration props, blueprints, and other loot
- **Jewelry** (rings and necklaces) can be found, appraised, and worn through the **hand mirror** for stat bonuses
- **Geodes** can be cracked open for gems used in crafting
- Recipe unlocks include useful town tools: **firewood**, **root remover**, **growth serum**, **hunting knife**, and **glow rings**
- **Sprinklers** help keep crops watered around your farm

### Town Journal

- **Guide** pages cover founding a town, mechanics, villagers, commands, and more (Voile narration supported)
- **Town** page lists all plots and villagers, with options to manage residents and buildings
- **Quests** page tracks active story quests, daily jobs, and board quests
- **Settings** page lets you adjust mod options and run repair tools when something goes wrong

## Getting started (quick path)

1. Craft a **town planning desk** at a workbench.
2. Use the desk to craft a **town charter** and **plot placement staff**.
3. Place the charter where you want your town center.
4. Talk to **Lyren the Elder** and accept the quest to **raise the inn**.
5. Stake the inn plot, bring materials to the sign, and finish the building.
6. Continue with the town hall, then take quests from villagers and visitors to grow.

Open the **Town Journal** anytime for the full walkthrough and detailed explanations of every system.
