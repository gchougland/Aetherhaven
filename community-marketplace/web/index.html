Aetherhaven community

Aetherhaven: Festivals of Orbis is a Hytale mod about founding a town, placing plot buildings, and growing a community of villagers with jobs, quests, and schedules. Download the mod on CurseForge.

This site hosts a public wiki guide and a community builds catalog below. Signed-in players can submit plot prefabs for moderation and share them with other towns once approved.
