About Aetherhaven

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Aetherhaven: Festivals of Orbis is a Hytale mod created by Hexvane. It adds town founding, plot-based building, villagers with schedules and quests, a treasury and tax system, shops, guild hall guards, and other mid-game town systems. The mod is available on CurseForge.

About this website

aetherhaven.net is the public home for two community features:

  • Wiki: player guides for Aetherhaven mechanics, villagers, addons, and developer integration. Much of this content mirrors the in-game Town Journal.
  • Community Builds: a catalog where players can browse, download, and share approved plot building prefabs for use in their towns.

New to the mod? Start with Getting Started in the wiki.

Contact
