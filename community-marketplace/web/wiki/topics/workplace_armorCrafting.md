# Armor Crafting

Apprentices make armor and guard kits for the town.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Blacksmith**, finish their work building and unlock **Armor Crafting** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Armor Crafting**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Armorer's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Blacksmithing** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Armor Crafting**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Choose Armor Crafting when saving the workplace, then open the bench and queue armor or an unlocked guard-kit recipe. Supply the listed metals and other ingredients through town storage.

## Work and output

Collect completed equipment from **Crafted Goods** or the task page. Equip a guard through their profile; making a kit does not equip it automatically.

## If work is waiting

Enable **Armor Crafting** in priorities. It is separate from Blacksmithing even though both use the Blacksmithing skill. Check bench tier and recipe unlocks.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
