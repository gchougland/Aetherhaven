**Pepper Ashford** is the town **chef**. She can visit the inn as soon as it is open. Once her **restaurant** is built, she cooks and sells food there, and townsfolk prefer eating at her tables instead of the inn hearth.


### Quests

#### Pepper's restaurant
Pepper wants a real kitchen, not just a shared kettle at Corin's.

1. Talk to Pepper at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the restaurant plot token from Pepper.
3. Stake and build the restaurant prefab.
4. Find Pepper at the restaurant and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Pepper's home
Once the restaurant is running, Pepper still wants a quiet house of her own.

1. Talk to Pepper and accept the housing quest.
2. Take the house plot token from Pepper.
3. Stake and build the house prefab.
4. Assign Pepper as resident on the house management block.
5. Find Pepper and close the quest in dialogue.

### Friendship rewards

Visit her after your friendship reaches each level to pick up the reward through dialogue.

- **Friendship 25:** teaches your town the **Chef's Utensil Roll** recipe.
- **Friendship 50:** teaches you to craft a **Cooking Bench**

### Restaurant upgrades

On the restaurant's town records page you can spend gold and ingredients on four upgrade paths:

- **Full bellies** — townsfolk stay full longer between meals
- **Quick service** — meals at the restaurant finish faster and fill more hunger
- **Chef's menu**: each level adds two dishes, and a resident who eats at the restaurant gets a short perk from one of them. Skewers help townsfolk work faster, salads slow the loss of energy, and pies slow the loss of fun. Each perk lasts about 8 hours.
- **Town provisions**: each level adds 5 Food Production, so the town can welcome more residents. The kitchen cooks what your farms and ranches bring in, so it never adds more food than they do.

### What she sells

The restaurant restocks **skewers**, **salads**, and **pies** (with a few produce sides) for villagers and tourists to buy from shop spots.

### Quest board

Once Pepper is a town resident, she can post **food fetch jobs** on the guild hall quest board. Bring the ingredients or cooked dishes she asks for and turn them in through her dialogue.
