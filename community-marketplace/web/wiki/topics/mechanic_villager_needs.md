# Villager needs

Every villager in town tracks three needs: **hunger**, **energy**, and **fun**. Each meter runs from empty to full. You can see them on the town records shelf in the inn or town hall, under the Needs tab.

## Why needs matter

Villagers slowly get hungrier, tire out, and want entertainment as the day goes on. When a meter is low, they look for the right place in town to fix it.

Needs also tie into **taxes**. Each morning, residents pay gold into the treasury. The amount depends partly on how comfortable they are. Comfortable villagers pay more. Keeping meters up is good for them and for your town wallet.

## Points of interest

Finished buildings add **spots** villagers can use: beds, benches, counters, workbenches, and similar blocks. Think of each spot as a small action in the world.

* **Beds** refill **energy** (including beds in houses at night).
* **Benches and seats**, including park benches, refill **fun**.
* **Kitchen counters, tables, and food storage** refill **hunger**.
* **Workbenches and shop counters** are for the job. They do not refill hunger, energy, or fun by themselves.

Different plots place different spots. A farm has farming benches. An inn has beds and meal space. A park has outdoor benches meant for relaxing.

## How villagers pick a spot

Villagers follow a daily schedule, but a very low meter can pull them away for a while. If **hunger** is critical, they head for food. If **energy** is low, they seek a bed. If **fun** is low, they look for a bench or social spot.

You do not need to click each spot yourself. Build the right plots and they will find them as the day runs.

## Houses at night

A **house** is optional. Villagers only need their **workplace** plot to hold a job in town.

If you assign someone to a house, they can sleep there at night. That sleep refills **energy**, which keeps them comfortable for the next morning tithe. Use a **park** when **fun** is the problem.

## Tips

* If **fun** is always the lowest meter, stake a **park** plot from your planning desk. Park benches are built for relaxing.
* Use the records shelf **Needs** tab to see everyone at a glance and teleport a villager to you if you want to check on them.
* Feasts and some town policies can change how much gold you earn from comfortable residents. Read **Treasury and taxes** for the money side.
