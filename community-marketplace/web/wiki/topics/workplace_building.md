# Builder's Yard

Apprentices travel to your town's construction projects and help build reachable pieces.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Builder**, finish their work building and unlock **Builder's Yard** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Builder's Yard**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Builder's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Building** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Builder's Yard**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Start a normal town construction project with a plot token. It appears on the yard's project board. Open the Builder's Workbench to review, reorder or pause projects. Supply their construction materials; Logistics workers can deliver from the warehouse to the plot sign.

## Work and output

Builders travel from the yard to the project. Several Apprentices can help, and higher Building skill and suitable equipment improve their pace. A finished project leaves the queue.

## If work is waiting

The yard creates buildings in the world, not stacks of building blocks. Check project materials and walking access to unfinished pieces. Use **Block Workshop** if you want planks, stairs or other blocks crafted as items.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
