# Sprinklers

Sprinklers water many farm cells at once on the **farm plot**. They are unlocked through friendship with the **Farmer**.

## How they work

* Place a sprinkler on the farm plot like other farm equipment.
* At **dawn** each day, an placed sprinkler refreshes crops in its radius automatically.
* You can also **use the block manually** to trigger a watering pass outside the daily schedule.

There are **four tiers** of sprinkler. Higher tiers cover a larger radius. Unlock tiers through the Farmer's friendship rewards and farm progression.

Read **Production wardrobe** if you configure which crops the farm generates, and **Villager needs** to see why well-fed townsfolk pay more in the morning tithe.
