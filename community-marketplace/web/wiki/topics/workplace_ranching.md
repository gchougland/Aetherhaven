# Ranching

A tended Ranching workplace adds **10 food points**, supporting **five residents**. It produces **no inventory items**.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Rancher**, finish their work building and unlock **Ranching** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Ranching**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Feeding Trough** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Ranching** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Ranching**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Craft a Feeding Trough at a Workbench from **5 tree trunks and 10 Plant Fiber**. Leave clear floor space around it inside the workplace. A cow and a chicken appear automatically; you do not need to bring animals or find a breeding pair.

## Work and output

An employed worker with Ranching enabled tends the site. Open **Food support and tending** to check care and readiness. The food bonus pauses if the workplace is neglected for a full simulated day.

## If work is waiting

Keep a clear route to the trough and space for both animals. Workplace animals stay nearby and cannot be captured or killed for resources. They disappear when the site is removed. The core Rancher's **Barn and owned animals** system has purchased animals, food and item output instead.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
