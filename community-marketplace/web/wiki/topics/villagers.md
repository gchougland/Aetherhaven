# Villagers

Each resident has a dedicated page with **quests**, **friendship rewards**, and a **weekly routine** pulled from their in-game schedule.

Villagers join town life in two ways: a **workplace** on their job plot (required for them to work there), and an optional **house** plot where they can sleep at night. See **Getting Started** for the difference between guests, visitors, and full residents.

Open a name on the left for quest steps and where to find that villager during the day. For gift preferences while playing, the in-game journal can hide spoiler lists until you choose to show them.

Every core villager offers a **home quest after their work-building recruitment quest**. Build a house, assign that villager on its Town Records Shelf and return to finish the quest. This includes the new specialist residents.

Open a villager's **Profile** for their portrait, reputation and discovered gift preferences. Locked reputation rewards stay hidden until you unlock them. Core workers show one segment per Mastery level. Townsfolk have no reputation or friendship rewards. Their profiles show profession skills, attributes and equipment. They earn Mastery XP through completed work, receive a skill point each level and two attribute points every five levels.
