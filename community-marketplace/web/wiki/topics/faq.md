# Frequently asked questions

Answers here are meant as a quick reference while you play or browse the site. For deeper detail, open **Mechanics** or **Villagers** from the directory.

## How do I install the mod?

Download **Aetherhaven: Festivals of Orbis** from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/aetherhaven) and install it like any other Hytale mod for your server or world.

## How do I submit a community build?

Sign in on [aetherhaven.net](https://aetherhaven.net) with **Sign in with Hytale**, open **My Submissions**, and upload your plot prefab with screenshots and a description. Moderators review submissions before they appear in the public catalog.

## Will the mod have *this* feature?

The answer is likely yes, but check the **Roadmap** page. Suggestions are always appreciated on the mod Discord.

## Where do I report bugs or suggest features?

Bugs and feature requests can be submitted on the mod Discord channel. Including your game version and whether you are single-player or on a server saves a lot of back-and-forth.

## Will saves break when the mod updates?

We try to keep data migrations safe, but **back up your world** before major updates, especially on long-lived servers.

## Can I design my own buildings instead of using the existing prefabs?

Integrated building tools are a planned feature for the mod. There is a good amount of configuration that needs to go into adding buildings at the moment. You can destroy or replace a building after it is constructed, but note that breaking or moving certain furniture or benches may prevent villagers from using them to fill their needs. For example, moving a bed can stop a villager from finding it. There is a build submissions channel on Discord if you would like your build to be featured in the main mod with credit.

## What data does the website collect?

The community site stores sign-in sessions, submission metadata, votes, and download counts as described on the [Privacy](https://aetherhaven.net/privacy.html) page.
