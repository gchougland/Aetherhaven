# Treasury and taxes

The **treasury block** inside the **town hall** holds your town's gold coins. You need the hall built before you can deposit, withdraw, and read tax lines in one place.

## Morning tithe

Each morning, villagers who count as **residents** pay a tithe based on how comfortable they are. Comfortable means their **hunger**, **energy**, and **fun** meters are in good shape. See **Villager needs** for how to keep those filled.

Tax collection runs while **at least one member of your town is online**. Gold still accrues for unloaded town chunks, but **no tithe runs while every town member is offline**.

Open the treasury UI to see coins add up. The second tab shows a breakdown of who paid what.

## Increasing income

Several town systems raise how much you earn beyond the base tithe, including:

* **Feasts** and feast policies
* The **founder statue**
* Other **town policies** shown in treasury menus

Read **Getting Started** for when Lyren asks you to raise the town hall, and **Inn pool and visitors** for guests who are not full residents.
