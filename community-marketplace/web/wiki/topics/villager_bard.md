**Elias Thornwood** is the town **bard**. He appears at the inn after the **guild hall** is built and plays music in the guild hall during the day.

Use **Show weekly schedule** below to see where Elias goes each day.

### Quests

#### Elias's home
Elias plays at the guild hall most days. He would like a quiet roof at night that belongs to him alone.

1. Take the house plot token from Elias.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Elias as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Elias and close the quest in dialogue.
