# Blacksmithing

Apprentices forge weapons, tools and unlocked profession equipment.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Blacksmith**, finish their work building and unlock **Blacksmithing** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Blacksmithing**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Blacksmith's Anvil** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Blacksmithing** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Blacksmithing**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Open the anvil's task page, choose a recipe and order a quantity, continuous crafting or a stock target. A recipe can require a stronger bench, a town unlock and a skilled worker. Bring its ingredients to town storage.

## Work and output

Finished orders go to **Crafted Goods**. Collect them on the task page or at the warehouse. A courier supplies ingredients when Logistics is enabled.

## If work is waiting

Read the selected recipe's requirements. A skilled smith still cannot use a missing or under-tier anvil, and an unlocked recipe still needs ingredients.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
