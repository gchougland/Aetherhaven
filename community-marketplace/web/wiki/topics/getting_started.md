# Getting Started

This page is a short path from an empty spot in the world to a town that earns gold and welcomes villagers.

## Start your town

1. Craft a **town planning desk** at a workbench.
2. Use the desk to craft a **town charter** and a **plot placement staff**.
3. Place the charter where you want the heart of the town to be. Your town gets a random name at first. Open the charter later to rename it.
4. **Lyren the Elder** appears. Talk to him to begin.

The charter marks your town borders. You can read more about moving or replacing it on the **Town charter** page under Mechanics.

## Your first quest: build the inn

Lyren asks you to **raise the inn**. In practice that means:

1. Talk to Lyren and accept the quest. He gives you the **inn plot token** (plot tokens for building quests come from the quest giver, not the planning desk).
2. Equip the **plot placement staff** and stake the inn footprint where it fits.
3. Bring the listed wood and stone to the plot sign and finish construction.

When the inn is done, an **innkeeper** moves in. The inn is your first real building and a home base for town records.

## How the inn helps

The inn is more than walls and a roof.

* **Visitors** can stay for a while. The inn holds up to two guests at a time. The guest list refreshes each morning unless you are working on a visitor quest that locks someone in place.
* The inn includes a **town records shelf** where you can check villager needs, invite friends to your town, and review gift history later on.

Read **Inn pool and visitors** under Mechanics if you want the full picture on guests.

## Money and taxes

Your town earns **gold coins** from a morning **tithe**. Each villager who counts as a resident pays based on how comfortable they are. Comfortable means their **hunger**, **energy**, and **fun** meters are in good shape.

Tax collection runs while at least one member of your town is online. Open the treasury after you build a town hall to see coins add up and read a breakdown of who paid what.

See **Treasury and taxes** under Mechanics for feast bonuses, policies, and other ways to earn more.

## Town hall and treasury

After the inn is finished, talk to Lyren again. He offers **raise the town hall**. Build it the same way you built the inn: take the plot token from the quest, use the placement staff, then bring materials to the sign.

The **town hall** holds the **treasury** block where your gold is stored. It also has another **town records shelf** and planning space. You need the hall if you want to deposit, withdraw, and study tax lines in one place.

## Who counts as a citizen

Villagers become part of your town in two different ways.

**Workplace (required for a job in town)**  
Build and finish the plot for their job (farm, shop, mill, and so on). Use the **management block** on that building to assign the villager to the workplace. Until they have a workplace, they are a guest or visitor, not a full resident tied to that job.

**House (optional)**  
A **house plot** is not required for them to work. If you build a house and register them on it, they can sleep there at night. Sleep refills **energy**, which also helps the gold they pay each morning. For **fun**, build a **park** (see below).

When you are ready to grow, take quest offers from villagers and visitors at the inn. Each new workplace you finish can add another resident.

## Tips

* Build a **park** if villagers often run low on **fun**. Park benches are the main way to refill fun. Open **Mechanics** and read the Villager needs page for hunger, energy, and fun in detail.
* Talk to villagers often and bring gifts if you want better prices, recipes, and milestone rewards. See **Reputation** under Mechanics.
* After the **town hall**, read **Mechanics** for the **guild hall and guards**, **quest board**, **tourist portal**, and other mid-game systems.
* Server hosts can use creative mode tools such as `/ah starterkit` to skip crafting. That is for testing, not the normal player path.
