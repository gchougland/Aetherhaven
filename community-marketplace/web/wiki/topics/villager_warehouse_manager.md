Mara can visit from **Town Level 2**. Build her **Warehouse** and finish her recruitment quest.

Open **Warehouse and deliveries** under Mechanics for the storage tabs, capacity upgrades, Supply Links and Field Ledger. **Logistics and couriers** explains the Townsfolk setup; no Logistics workplace is needed.

## Storage and deliveries

Use the **Warehouse Ledger Desk** to open town storage. Search updates the item list while you type. General storage holds supplies, Crafted Goods holds finished workplace orders, and Building Outputs shows goods kept in each production source's own store. Locked choices explain their requirements. Use Store matching to deposit carried kinds already in General storage; production stores keep their own capacity limits.

Enable **Logistics** for employed Townsfolk on the journal's **Work** page. Couriers carry reserved ingredients from storage and production buildings to workplaces. Completed crafting orders go to **Crafted Goods**. Supplies remain at their source until pickup. A full destination pauses the delivery. Each material is fetched from one place that holds enough of it: the production building or furnace that has it, otherwise general storage, which is collected at a ledger desk in the Warehouse. If that place is emptied before the courier arrives, the delivery is planned again.

Choose **Building upgrades** from town storage to buy more storage room. Each expansion needs the previous expansion, the shown Town Level and treasury gold. Apprentice recipes only begin when the workplace has its bench, a qualified worker and the required supplies.


### Friendship rewards

- **Friendship 25:** Warehouse Supply Link recipe. Craft it at the Town Planning Desk and place it within the normal chest range of your workbenches. It draws from general storage, crafted goods and building outputs, keeping courier reservations safe. Open the link to browse the warehouse. Collect barn milk into buckets first.
- **Friendship 50:** Quartermaster Pack recipe.
- **Friendship 75:** Field Ledger.
