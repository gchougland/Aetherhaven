**Lyra Fairhollow** runs the **guild hall** once it is built. Adventurers gather here, and you can hire them as **guards** who patrol and fight hostile mobs. See **Guild hall and guards** under Mechanics for patrol routes and the command post.

Use **Show weekly schedule** below to see where Lyra goes each day.

### Quests

#### Raise the guild hall
Lyra wants a proper guild hall where adventurers can sign on and guards can be hired.

1. Finish the **town hall** first, then talk to Lyra at the inn.
2. Take the guild hall plot token from Lyra.
3. Stake and build the guild hall prefab.
4. Find Lyra at the guild hall and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Lyra's home
Lyra runs the guild hall from dawn to dusk. She would like a quiet roof at night that belongs to her alone.

1. Take the house plot token from Lyra.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Lyra as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Lyra and close the quest in dialogue.
