**Rivet Cogsworth** is a robot mechanic freed from rust with a bath of black oil. Rivet sells metal and gear from the Rusted Expanse once the **mechanics shop** is built. Rivet appears at the inn after you oil the rusty robot and talk with them.

Use **Show weekly schedule** below to see where Rivet goes each day. Gift likes and loves are listed under **Show gift preferences**.

### Quests

#### Rivet's rescue
Find a rusty robot in the Rusted Expanse, break it for the block, and throw it into oil. Talk with Rivet when they wake up.

#### Rivet's mechanics shop
Help Rivet open a shop where the town can buy metal and gear.

1. Talk to Rivet at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the shop blueprint from Rivet.
3. Use the blueprint at your plot crafting bench to learn the mechanics shop.
4. Craft a shop plot token and build the mechanics shop prefab.
5. Find Rivet at the shop and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Rivet's home
Even a freshly oiled robot deserves a quiet place to rest.

1. Take the house plot token from Rivet.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Rivet as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Rivet and close the quest in dialogue.
