# Farming

A tended Farming workplace adds **10 food points**, enough to support **five residents**. It produces **no inventory items**.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Farmer**, finish their work building and unlock **Farming** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Farming**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Farmer's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Farming** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Farming**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Leave at least **four clear dirt, grass or tilled-soil spaces** at the marked floor with room above for wheat. Townsfolk prepare the ground and plant wheat themselves: you do not need seeds or existing crops. Keep the bench and growing spaces inside the marked bounds.

## Work and output

Open **Food support and tending** to see the growing-space count and last care. Workers revisit every six game hours. After a full simulated day without tending, the food bonus pauses until care resumes.

## If work is waiting

The screen identifies missing equipment, insufficient growing space, worker requirements and path problems. Selecting a crop or bench corner includes its base, but always review the marked floor and height. Irienne's **Wheat for the Town** quest walks through setup. For harvested items, use **Farm and Scarecrow Fields** instead.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
