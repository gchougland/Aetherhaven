# Farming

Each edible crop growing in a tended Farming workplace adds **1 food point**. Every **two food points support one resident**: 4 crops support 2 residents, and 20 crops support 10. Empty soil does not count. It produces **no inventory items**.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Farmer**, finish their work building and unlock **Farming** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Farming**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Farmer's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Farming** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Farming**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Leave at least **four clear dirt, grass or tilled-soil spaces** with room above for wheat. The bench and growing spaces can be up to **two blocks below** the marked floor, so marking corners on fences still includes the ground between them. Townsfolk prepare the ground and keep planting wheat until every clear soil space has a crop: you do not need seeds or existing crops. They leave paths, buildings and other plants alone. Keep them inside the marked horizontal bounds.

## Work and output

Open **Food support and tending** to see the actual crop count, clear spaces still to plant, food support and last care. Workers tend the first four crops, then keep planting the remaining clear soil between care visits. Removing crops immediately reduces support once the loaded area is inspected. Workers revisit for care every six game hours. After a full simulated day without tending, the food bonus pauses until care resumes.

## If work is waiting

The screen identifies missing equipment, insufficient growing space, worker requirements and path problems. Check the included heights shown on screen if the ground is more than two blocks below your selection. Irienne's **Wheat for the Town** quest walks through setup. For harvested items, use **Farm and Scarecrow Fields** instead.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
