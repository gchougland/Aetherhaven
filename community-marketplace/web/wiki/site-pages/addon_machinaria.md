# Machinaria

**Machinaria** adds a rusty scrapyard fragment and crafting gadgets. It also ships an **optional Aetherhaven integration**—you do not need Machinaria for Aetherhaven, and you do not need Aetherhaven for Machinaria’s core loop.

## What you get with both mods

- **Rivet Cogsworth**, a robot mechanic freed from rust with black oil
- Inn unlock after the rescue, then shop and house quests
- A **mechanics shop** selling metal and gear from the Rusted Expanse (vanilla metal sets plus Machinaria blocks)
- Quest board fetch jobs and a journal guide page for Rivet

## How to unlock Rivet

1. Install **Machinaria** and **Aetherhaven** on the same world or server.
2. In the Rusted Expanse, find a rusty robot, break it for the block, and throw it into oil.
3. Talk with Rivet when they wake, then meet them at the inn.
4. Accept shop and house quests from Rivet at the inn, and build the mechanics shop / house as usual.

Full quest steps, schedule, and gift tastes: **[Rivet Cogsworth](?topic=villager_mechanic)**.
