The mine's storage page says why he is not swinging in place of "Waiting for the Miner". If it happens again, that line is the thing to report.
- [ ] **N73 · Confirm workers no longer approach underground mineshafts from above ground.** A miner used to accept any reachable stand, including the surface over a shallow cut or a ledge beside it, and stayed put if he already had reach from there. The isolated mineshaft check passes with this rule on all 28 layout and entrance combinations; a real hillside shaft has not been watched.
- [ ] **N75 · Confirm workers only visit buildings that hold what they need.** The owner's reading was right in part: a delivery could be split across buildings, and a pickup was never rechecked. Each material is now fetched whole from one place that holds enough of it: a production building or furnace that has it, otherwise the warehouse's general storage. It is split across places only when no single place has enough. A delivery whose source was emptied, moved or removed is cancelled within two seconds and prepared again from wherever the material now is. General storage is collected at a desk inside the Warehouse when one is loaded, not at whichever desk is nearest. A barn's animal feed is no longer fetched from that same barn. Covered by unit tests; couriers have not been watched.

Seren plants the tree, sings it up with nature magic, fells it and carries the wood, sapling, sap and sticks home, and the stone regrows it for free. The slot picker, Building Staff marking, saved plans, the separate start step and the stuck changed tree state are gone. Life Essence blessings double one harvest.
