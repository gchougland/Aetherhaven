# Purification powder

Purification powder lets you remove enemy spawn points inside generated structures. It is unlocked by befriending **Serah**, the **Priestess**.

## How to use it

1. Craft or obtain purification powder after the unlock.
2. Hold the powder in your hand. Small spinning mob markers appear where periodic spawns would occur in that structure.
3. Use the powder on a marker to remove that spawn point permanently.

This is separate from **jewelry** cleansing rolls. Powder targets world structure spawners, not item stats.

Read the **Priestess** villager page for her Gaia altar quests and friendship rewards.
