**Vaelith Prismshade** is an ancient **crystal keeper** who sells crystals and gems. You must **find and free her in the world** before she will visit the inn. After the rescue, she can open a crystal shop in town.


### Quests

#### Vaelith's rescue
While exploring, you may find Vaelith trapped in crystal. Free her to complete this quest. She will then start appearing at the inn.

#### Vaelith's crystal shop
Vaelith wants a proper shop where she can share her crystal collection with the town.

1. Talk to Vaelith at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the shop **blueprint** from Vaelith.
3. Use the blueprint at your **plot crafting bench** to learn the crystal shop.
4. Craft a shop plot token and build the crystal shop prefab.
5. Find Vaelith at the shop and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Vaelith's home
Even an ancient keeper of crystals deserves a quiet place to rest between the light of her stones.

1. Take the house plot token from Vaelith.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Vaelith as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Vaelith and close the quest in dialogue.
