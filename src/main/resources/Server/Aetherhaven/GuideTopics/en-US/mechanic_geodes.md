# Geodes

Geodes are chunky rocks found rarely when mining ores and crystals. They can hold ores, crystal shards, and sometimes gems inside.

## Cracking geodes

Take geodes to **Garren Vale** at the **blacksmith shop**. He cracks them open for a gold fee. Rewards vary by geode type.

If your friendship with Garren is high enough, he teaches you to craft the **Geode Anvil**. That bench lets you open geodes yourself without paying the blacksmith each time.
