Kael can visit from **Town Level 4**. Build his **Hunter's Lodge** and finish his recruitment quest.

## The arena

The **Hunt Beacon** marks the centre of the arena. Keep a clear **9 by 9** floor around it with room overhead. The beacon must stay inside the completed lodge plot.

Unlock creatures with treasury gold and samples of their drops. Choose **Start** to begin a run. Kael fights one creature at a time, collects its spoils and faces progressively stronger opponents. Runs never begin automatically and do not progress while the world is unattended.

Skeletons provide bones and linen scraps. Frost undead also provide Ice Essence. Burnt and incandescent undead provide Cindercloth and Fire Essence. Outlanders provide Shadoweave. Void Crawlers and the larger Void Spawn provide Void Essence and can drop Voidhearts. Spoils follow the creature's normal drop chances and amounts, excluding weapons, armor and tools.

Collected spoils stay safe when a run ends. Open the lodge's **Production Storage** to browse and collect spoils, or collect them through the town warehouse. Make space when the spoils racks fill. Couriers can use lodge supplies for workplace orders.

When creating or editing a Hunter's Lodge, place and mark **Production Storage** inside its bounds. The building editor requires this storage before saving.

## Training

Spend treasury gold on vitality, power, field dressing, Second Wind and larger spoils racks between runs. Training improves Kael's survival and lets him complete longer runs.
