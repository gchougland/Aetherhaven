**Jack Hollow** can be found by opening a coffin during October. This follows the server's calendar, rather than the seasons in your world.

Speak to him to unlock his visits at the Inn. Build his **Spook House** to welcome him as a permanent resident.

Jack stays in town throughout the year once he has joined. He does not award a Memory.

### Hide and seek

Ask Jack to play. His pumpkin will appear on a clear floor inside one of your town buildings. Use the pickup prompt, then carry it back and tell him you found it.

A successful game gives **3 Halloween candies and 5 gold**. Each player can earn the reward once per game day. Make room for both rewards before returning the pumpkin.

For a hint, use **/ah halloween head** to name the building hiding his pumpkin.

Jack plays one game at a time. You can ask him to stop your game and try again without using your daily reward. Players who manage town buildings can also stop an abandoned game. Pumpkins from cancelled games cannot earn rewards.

If the hiding building is moved or removed, the game ends safely. Keep a clear floor with a roof and space to reach the pumpkin in your buildings.
