**Elara Willowmere** becomes available at the Inn at **Town Level 3**. Build her **Clinic** to give your residents a place to recover.

### Clinic care

Talk to Elara and choose **Let us check the clinic**. The medicine cabinet accepts **Lesser Health Potions** from your inventory. Each bottle supplies **80 medicine**, and the cabinet holds **800**. Medicine is spent only when someone receives healing.

Bring injured guards into the clinic with **Follow**, then ask them to **Wait Here**. Elara treats one nearby resident at a time, seeing the most seriously injured first. Both people must remain inside the clinic and close together. Twelve quiet seconds restore up to forty health. Fighting and new injuries interrupt treatment. Elara also needs to take breaks for food and rest.

The clinic cannot revive the dead. Visit the **Gaia Altar** for revival.

Guards also recover slowly when resting safely. After thirty quiet seconds, they recover a little health every ten seconds. Clinic medicine provides faster recovery. The care screen shows Elara's availability and treatment progress while it is open.

### Friendship

Elara offers care packages at 25, 50 and 75 reputation. The first helps stock the clinic; later packages contain stronger potions for your journeys.
