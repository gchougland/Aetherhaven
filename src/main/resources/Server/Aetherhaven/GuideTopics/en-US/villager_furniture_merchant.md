**Cap'n Clive** sells decoration props from his **furniture shop** once it is built. Talk to him to browse what he has for sale that day. He appears at the inn after **Vex's market stall** is finished.


### Quests

#### Clive's furniture shop
Cap'n Clive wants a proper shop where folk can browse his decoration props.

1. Talk to Clive at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the shop **blueprint** from Clive.
3. Use the blueprint at your **plot crafting bench** to learn the furniture shop.
4. Craft a shop plot token and build the furniture shop prefab.
5. Find Clive at the shop and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Clive's home
Even a traveling prop seller deserves a quiet berth on dry land.

1. Take the house plot token from Clive.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Clive as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Clive and close the quest in dialogue.

## Gifts

Clive loves shells, star fish, treasure, and good fish. He likes cooked food, jewelry, gems, and winter holiday treats. Keep trash, bones, void bits, and coffins away from his counter.
