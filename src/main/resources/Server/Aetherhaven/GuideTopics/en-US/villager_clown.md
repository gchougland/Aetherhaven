**Bozo Bleak** is the town clown. Win him from the carnival wheel, then speak to him about building his tent.

### Friendship rewards

Visit after reaching each friendship level to claim the reward through dialogue. Plans are learned by the town and crafted by Apprentices.

- **Friendship 25:** Clown Nose Pattern for the town wardrobe.
- **Friendship 50:** Balloons plans, requiring Crafting 3.
- **Friendship 75:** Red Balloon Hat Pattern for the town wardrobe.
