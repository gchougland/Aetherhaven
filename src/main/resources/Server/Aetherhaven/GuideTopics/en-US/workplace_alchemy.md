# Alchemy

Apprentices brew queued potion recipes from plants, petals, mushrooms and other supplies.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Alchemist**, finish their work building and unlock **Alchemy** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Alchemy**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Alchemist's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Alchemy** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Alchemy**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Unlock Alchemy after Orin has settled in his Laboratory. Open the workplace's bench, choose a recipe, set its order mode and provide the ingredients through town storage. The Druid's Spirit Garden, Mycologist and Berry Patch can provide ingredients.

## Work and output

Completed recipe items go to **Crafted Goods**. Employed Logistics workers deliver reserved supplies. The recipe screen shows missing ingredients, skill, equipment and town unlocks.

## If work is waiting

This is Apprentice recipe work. To discover ingredient effects, play the heat minigame or refill a reusable flask yourself, read **Alchemy and flasks** and visit the Laboratory cauldron.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
