# Jewelry Crafting

Apprentices make unlocked jewelry recipes, including equipment learned through friendship.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Merchant**, finish their work building and unlock **Jewelry Crafting** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Jewelry Crafting**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Jewelry Crafting Bench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Jewelry** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Jewelry Crafting**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Unlock Jewelry Crafting through the Merchant and mark a workplace around a Jewelry Crafting Bench. Queue a recipe and supply its listed materials. Place an **Appraisal Bench** inside the same workplace if you want new jewelry to arrive appraised.

## Work and output

Collect completed jewelry from **Crafted Goods** or the task page. The optional Appraisal Bench handles appraisal; it does not replace the required Jewelry Crafting Bench.

## If work is waiting

Read the recipe's Jewelry skill and unlock requirements. Some plans come from villager friendship rewards. See **Jewelry** for wearing, inspecting and appraising the finished items.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
