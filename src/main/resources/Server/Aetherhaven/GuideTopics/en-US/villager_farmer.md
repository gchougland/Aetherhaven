**Irienne Mossmark** is usually found at **the farm plot** when they are on shift.

### Quests

#### Irienne's farm plot
Give Irienne fenced ground and a bench so she can stop dreaming of rows on an inn floor.

1. Take the farm plot token from Irienne.
2. Stake and build the farm plot prefab.
3. Find Irienne at the farming bench and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Irienne's home
Irienne wants roots under rafters that belong to her, not borrowed floorboards by the kettle.

1. Take the house plot token from Irienne.
2. Place and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Irienne as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Irienne and close the quest in dialogue.

### Field work preview

Plant crops on tilled beds inside the farm building's area. Open farm storage, select the crops you want as its outputs, then choose **Review planted beds**. The map shows planted crops and which are ripe. Choose **Start field work** to let Irienne tend those beds during work hours.

This replaces the farm's old automatic output. Stored items and upgrades are kept. **Collect all** can still recover old stock, including Life Essence. Every harvest also brings back Life Essence, as much as the crop drops and at least one, while the farm has room for it. You do not need to select it as an output. Collect it with **Collect all** or from Building Outputs in the Warehouse. Harvest amounts follow the planted crop. Seeds are not collected.

Irienne waters growing beds, harvests ripe crops and carries each harvest to the farm's storage. Native crops regrow after harvesting. Produce only becomes available after she delivers it. A full store pauses further harvesting, so empty it or let Couriers collect the materials they need.

You plant the first crops yourself. If you move or replace crops, return to the review and choose **Update tended beds**. Keep up to 64 planted beds in the farm. Purchased material slots let you select more crop types. Speed upgrades shorten attended tending and harvest work; crop growth still follows native farming, watering and light.

Each crop also needs the Farmer's Mastery shown in storage. Talk to Irienne to train her with Verdant Catalysts. This is separate from an Apprentice's Farming skill. Apprentices make seeds and saplings at Crafting workplaces using Farming skill, with the required level and Life Essence cost shown on each recipe.

Crops inside Apprentice workplaces are excluded from the core Farmer's fields. Apprentice farms support town population and never produce item output. Core field harvesting does not grant extra food support. Worker paths, visual quality and survival throughput are still being tested.

### Friendship rewards

Visit them after your friendship reaches each level to pick up the reward through dialogue.

- **Friendship 25:** teaches you to craft **Sprinkler Iron**
- **Friendship 50:** teaches you to craft **Sprinkler Thorium**
- **Friendship 75:** teaches you to craft **Sprinkler Cobalt**
- **Friendship 100:** teaches you to craft **Sprinkler Adamantite**
