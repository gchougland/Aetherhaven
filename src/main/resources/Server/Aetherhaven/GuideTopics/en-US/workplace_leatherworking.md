# Leatherworking

Apprentices turn hides into leather and make unlocked leather goods and profession equipment.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Leatherworker**, finish their work building and unlock **Leatherworking** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Leatherworking**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Tanning Rack** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Leatherworking** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Leatherworking**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Recruit Hazel to her Tannery and unlock Leatherworking. Queue a tanning or leather-goods recipe at the rack. Light, medium and heavy hides need Leatherworking **1, 3 and 5** respectively. Supply the listed materials through town storage.

## Work and output

Collect finished leather and equipment from **Crafted Goods** or the task page. An equipped Leatherworker's Roll and a Stitching Frame furnishing can improve work speed.

## If work is waiting

Check the recipe's own skill and equipment requirements. Portable backpacks are crafted and upgraded directly through **Hazel's backpack workshop**; read **Backpacks** for that separate service.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
