# Mining

Apprentices excavate real tunnels, stairs and rooms, then carry mined drops back to their base.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Miner**, finish their work building and unlock **Mining** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Mining**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Mining** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Mining**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Use the Workbench to open the **Mineshaft planner**. Each map cell is three blocks wide. Select a blue base cell or a green passage, choose a direction and a length in three-block steps, then choose a level tunnel, stairs or a 9 by 9 room. **Review terrain**, then **Add reviewed tunnel** to authorize permanent digging.

## Work and output

Enable Mining for an employed, paid townsfolk. A worker carries 25 items at Mining 1 and 10 more for each additional level. Collect from **Open output storage** in the planner or **Building Outputs** at the warehouse. A core mine is not required.

## If work is waiting

Use floor controls to inspect connected passages. Seven-block stairs need at least nine blocks of length. Beams, torches, lanterns and rails need their named materials in General storage. Rails are not placed in rooms. Keep entrances clear and check terrain warnings and ore skill requirements. Removing an order does not restore dug terrain; remove connected branches first.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
