# Inn pool and visitors

The **inn** can host visitors who are not full-time residents. Visitors appear in the inn commons, offer quests, and may later ask for workplaces of their own.

## Capacity and refresh

* The inn holds up to **two visitors** at a time.
* The guest list **rerolls at dawn** each day unless a visitor is locked by an active quest.
* If you have accepted a visitor's quest, that visitor **stays until you finish** the quest line they started.

The inn also includes a **town records shelf** for needs, invites, and gift history. Read **Getting Started** for how the inn fits into founding a town.

## Full residents vs visitors

A villager with a finished **workplace** plot assigned on the management block counts as a resident for taxes and schedules. Visitors at the inn are guests until you build their job plot and assign them.

## Debugging

If the inn's visitors do not appear or look wrong, try running **`/ah villager fixinn`** (see **Commands** under Mechanics).
