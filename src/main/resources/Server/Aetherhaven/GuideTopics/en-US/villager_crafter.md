Tobin can visit from **Town Level 3**. Complete his **Artisan Workshop**, then unlock Crafting workplaces through his dialogue.

Mark a workplace with the Building Staff and place an ordinary **Workbench** inside it. Employ an Apprentice, enable Crafting on the journal's Work page, and add orders through the workplace controls. The recipe list includes recipes registered for the ordinary workbench, including recipes supplied by other packs. Each recipe keeps its materials and bench requirements.

Seed and sapling plans use **Life Essence** and the Apprentice's **Farming** skill. The task screen shows the required Farming level for each plant.

Couriers bring ingredients and return completed goods to the warehouse. Bench upgrades and required recipe unlocks still apply. A **Pattern Cabinet** improves attended Crafting work speed without replacing the workbench.
