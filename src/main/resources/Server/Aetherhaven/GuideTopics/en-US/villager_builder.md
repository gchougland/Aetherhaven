**Rowan Ridgecraft** is the town **builder**. Once his hut is finished, he travels to plots that are still assembling during work hours and helps them rise faster while he works at the sign.


### Quests

#### Rowan's builder's hut
Give Rowan a proper hut where he can plan builds and help the town grow.

1. Talk to Rowan at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the builder's hut plot token from Rowan.
3. Stake and build the builder's hut prefab.
4. Find Rowan at the hut and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Rowan's home
Even a builder who lives for good architecture deserves a quiet place to rest.

1. Take the house plot token from Rowan.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Rowan as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Rowan and close the quest in dialogue.
