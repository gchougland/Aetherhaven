# Player shop and shop spots

**Shop spot** blocks live inside finished building plots. They let NPCs and players sell items in the world instead of only through dialogue menus.

## NPC shops

Villager shops — **Vex's general store**, the Florist, Pyrotechnic, Crystal Keeper, and others — restock at **dawn** from loot tables. Each spot shows a floating item display; the stall's facing rotates the display.

* **F (use)** — open the buy panel with quantity controls (±1, ±10, max).
* **Look-at HUD** — shows item name, price, and stock while you aim at a stocked spot.

Vex now runs a **general store** selling furniture, blocks, and consumables through shop spots in his market stall and related buildings.

## Your own shop

After **Vex's market stall** is built, Vex offers **Your Own Shop**:

1. Take the **player shop** plot token from Vex.
2. Stake and build the player shop prefab.
3. Find Vex and close the quest in dialogue.

The player shop includes **shop spot** blocks and a **shop safe** that holds gold from your sales.

### Listing items

* **Right click a shop spot** while holding an item — list it for sale (player-controlled spots only).
* **Right click again** on your own listing — remove it from the spot.

Buyers press **F** on stocked spots to purchase. You receive a notification when someone buys your listing, and gold collects in the **shop safe**.

### Who shops

**Villagers** and **tourists** browse player shop spots and buy listed items when they visit town. Price and stock matter — empty spots are skipped.

## Permissions

Town members need permission to sell on player shop spots. The town owner can adjust member permissions from the town records shelf.

See **Plot tokens** under Mechanics for crafting plot tokens at the plot crafting bench.
