# Crafting

Apprentices make supplies, repair items, equipment and unlocked seed or sapling recipes.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Crafter**, finish their work building and unlock **Crafting** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Crafting**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Crafting** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Crafting**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Recruit Tobin to his Artisan Workshop and unlock Crafting. Use the workplace's ordinary Workbench to queue recipes with a quantity, continuous crafting or a stock target. Supply ingredients from town storage.

## Work and output

Finished goods appear in **Crafted Goods** and on the task page. Seed and sapling recipes are also ordered here, but use the worker's **Farming** skill for their recipe requirement. Keep **Crafting** enabled for that worker.

## If work is waiting

Check the skill named by the recipe, its bench tier and unlock. A Joiner's Toolbox speeds Crafting when equipped. A Pattern Cabinet improves attended work inside the site; it does not replace the bench or waive recipe requirements.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
