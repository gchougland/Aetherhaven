**Ivy Bloomwell** sells plants and flowers from her **flower shop** once it is built. She appears at the inn after **Vex's market stall** is finished.


### Quests

#### Ivy's flower shop
Ivy wants a proper shop where she can share her plants with the town.

1. Talk to Ivy at the inn and accept the quest.
2. Take the shop **blueprint** from Ivy.
3. Use the blueprint at your **plot crafting bench** to learn the flower shop.
4. Craft a shop plot token and build the flower shop prefab.
5. Find Ivy at the shop and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Ivy's home
Even a florist who talks to her plants all day deserves a quiet place to rest.

1. Take the house plot token from Ivy.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Ivy as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Ivy and close the quest in dialogue.
