**Rowan Mossweald** becomes available at the Inn at **Town Level 4**. Build his **Conservatory** and return to him to complete his recruitment quest. The Conservatory is available in the Core style and includes planted crystal beds and local storage.

## Start tending beds

Open the Conservatory's storage cupboard. Select the alchemical plants you want as outputs, then choose **Review ingredient beds**. The map shows planted beds, their growth and any Mastery requirement. Choose **Start field work** to let Rowan tend them during his work hours.

Rowan grows the three tiers of **Health, Mana and Stamina** plants on **Crystal Soil**. He needs Mastery **1** for the first tier, **4** for the second and **7** for the third. Train him with Verdant Catalysts through his Profile. Output unlocks and purchased output slots also apply.

Rowan tends growing plants, harvests ripe plants and carries each ingredient to Conservatory storage. Established plants regrow after his harvest. Ingredients become available after delivery. A full cupboard pauses harvesting until you make room. Couriers can collect stored ingredients needed by Apprentice brewing tasks.

You can plant additional alchemical seeds on Crystal Soil inside the Conservatory. Replace missing plants yourself, then choose **Update tended beds**. Keep no more than **64** planted beds and leave clear paths between the plants and storage. Higher speed upgrades shorten attended work; plant growth still takes time.

## Seeds and older gardens

Seed and sapling recipes are available at Crafting workplaces and use the Apprentice's **Farming** skill. Alchemical seed tiers need Farming **1, 4 and 7**. Check each recipe for its Life Essence cost and equipment requirements.

Herb Garden workplaces have been removed. The Druid grows ingredients directly in the Conservatory. Existing plants remain in the world. Unused supplies stay in storage, and couriers return supplies that were already picked up.

These plants produce potion ingredients. They do not provide town food support.


### Friendship rewards

- **Friendship 25:** Botanist's Tool Roll recipe for town blacksmithing.
- **Friendship 75:** Verdant Catalyst recipe.
