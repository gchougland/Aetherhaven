Mara can visit from **Town Level 2**. Build her **Warehouse** and finish her recruitment quest.

## Storage and deliveries

Use the **Warehouse Ledger Desk** to open town storage. Search updates the item list while you type. General storage holds supplies, Crafted Goods holds finished workplace orders, and production storage shows goods delivered by production workers. Only output slots a building has unlocked are listed there.

Enable **Logistics** for employed Townsfolk on the journal's **Work** page. Couriers carry reserved ingredients from storage and production buildings to workplaces, then bring finished goods back. Supplies remain at their source until pickup. A full destination pauses the delivery. Each material is fetched from one place that holds enough of it: the production building or furnace that has it, otherwise general storage, which is collected at a ledger desk in the Warehouse. If that place is emptied before the courier arrives, the delivery is planned again.

Choose **Building upgrades** from town storage to buy more storage room. Each expansion needs the previous expansion, the shown Town Level and treasury gold. Apprentice recipes only begin when the workplace has its bench, a qualified worker and the required supplies.


### Friendship rewards

- **Friendship 25:** Warehouse Supply Link recipe. Craft it at the Town Planning Desk and place it within the normal chest range of your workbenches. It draws from general storage, crafted goods and building outputs, keeping courier reservations safe. Open the link to browse the warehouse. Collect barn milk into buckets first.
- **Friendship 50:** Quartermaster Pack recipe.
- **Friendship 75:** Field Ledger.
