Sera can visit from **Town Level 4**. Complete her **Tannery**, then unlock Leatherworking workplaces through her dialogue.

Mark a workplace with the Building Staff and place a **Tanning Rack** inside it. Employ an Apprentice with Leatherworking skill and enable that profession on the journal's Work page. Add orders through the workplace controls.

The recipe list includes the tanning rack's registered recipes and town plans for leather equipment and courier satchels. Every recipe shows its skill, equipment and ingredient requirements. Couriers deliver supplies and return finished goods to the warehouse.

A **Stitching Frame** improves attended Leatherworking speed. It does not replace the tanning rack. Train and equip your Apprentice through their profile.
