**Gorruk Stonevein** is usually found at **the miner's hut** when they are on shift.

### Mastery and tools

Gorruk's own production requires both his Mastery and an equipped Pickaxe. Talk to him to train Mastery with Verdant Catalysts and equip a better tool from your inventory. He starts with a Crude Pickaxe. Iron needs Mastery 3 and Copper; Thorium and Cobalt need Mastery 5 and 6 respectively and Iron; Adamantite needs Mastery 8 and Thorium or Cobalt. Higher tools satisfy lower requirements. See **Mastery and training** for the full table. These requirements are separate from the Mining skill used by Apprentices below.

### Apprentice mineshafts

Unlock Mining through the Miner's upgrades. Hold the Building Staff, middle click and choose **Plan mineshaft**. Click the ground just in front of the rock, choose the digging direction and length, then **Save and review**. The tunnel starts one block ahead of that ground. You do not need to reach its far end or dig it out first. Keep the site inside town and outside building plots. Put a normal Workbench beside the entrance path. Enable Mining for a paid Apprentice in the journal's Work page.

Choose Straight, Branches, Stairs up or Stairs down, then review the terrain. Passages are three blocks wide and three blocks high. Length ranges from 3 to 32 blocks, or up to 16 for Branches. The workplace width is set automatically. Stairs change height every four rows, up to two blocks, starting at the chosen ground. Keep the clicked row and the two rows behind it clear for the approach. Gold squares contain rock to excavate; green squares are clear. **Start reviewed tunnel** authorizes permanent removal of that reviewed rock. Supporting floors are kept.

Use **Direction and length** to adjust the plan from its current entrance. Hold a Building Staff and choose **Choose new start** to move the entrance. Editing or cancelling a setup does not change saved work. Saving a different area stops the old order until you review and start its replacement. Collected material must be delivered before changing the site.

An Apprentice walks to the face, swings a pickaxe and removes one block at a time. Native drops are carried to the core mine's storage before becoming available. They are not renewable nodes. Full storage pauses digging. Couriers can collect delivered materials from that same mine storage.

Small displays of the collected resources mark material waiting for transport. They are not loose items for the player to pick up. If a miner is interrupted, the saved material stays at the last carrying location. A returning miner must reach it before resuming delivery.

Stronger ores need greater Mining skill: Iron 3, Silver and Gold 4, Thorium 5, Cobalt 7, Adamantite 9 and Mithril 10. Copper and ordinary rock need skill 1. These thresholds and work times are still being balanced.

Use **Pause saved tunnel** to stop excavation. Collected material can still be delivered. Player edits pause the reviewed order and require a new review. Water, furnishings, building plots, unavailable terrain and removed permissions can also stop work. Blueprints and buildings still under construction are protected too. Changing a workplace cannot discard collected material.

Choose optional Supports, Torches, or Supports and torches before starting. The review shows the exact Softwood Beam and Crude Torch costs. After digging and delivering the last rock, the worker adds these fittings inside the cleared passage. Each piece uses one available item from General or Output town storage when it is placed. Reserved materials are left for their existing orders. Missing supplies or an occupied position pause placement; add supplies or clear the obstruction to continue. Fittings require building and storage permission and never replace existing blocks. Changing the review does not change a running order until you start the replacement.

The task screen also opens the normal station upgrade controls. Upgrading this workbench does not currently shorten excavation time.

### Quests

#### Gorruk's home
Gorruk wants a proper house off the inn floor, same as anyone else with a plot token.

1. Take the house plot token from Gorruk.
2. Stake and build the house prefab.
3. Assign Gorruk as resident on the house management block.
4. Find Gorruk and close the quest in dialogue.

#### Gorruk's miners hut
Give Gorruk a proper shed and bench so he can haul stone and ore without crowding the inn.

1. Take the miners hut plot token from Gorruk.
2. Stake and build the miners hut prefab.
3. Find Gorruk at the workbench and close the quest in dialogue.

### Updating an older mine

Existing mines keep their original production until you choose to update them. In Creative mode, aim the **Building Editor Staff** at your completed mine and press **Use** to review its new node layout. You must belong to the town and have permission to manage construction.

The review starts with node locations from the currently installed building design. You can also choose **Choose cells in this mine** to make a local layout, even if the original design has no nodes. Left click plain rock, ore or soil to select a cell. Right click a selected cell to remove it. Ore previews show your selection, and the staff display shows your current controls. Press **Use** to review the plan, or the displayed **Discard selection** key to cancel.

Review each location on the plan, or choose **Show in world** for temporary markers. The arrows let you inspect every node, including nodes above one another. Local changes apply only to this placed mine and do not change the downloaded building design. Putting the staff away hides the previews. Leaving the mine area, disconnecting or remaining idle for 30 minutes discards the selection.

**Apply node layout** checks the blocks again and keeps your building, stored resources, selected outputs and purchased upgrades. It does not paste a fresh building. Changed blocks, furniture, storage or a lack of standing space prevent the update. The Miner must work at the nodes to produce resources after conversion. Updating a marketplace download alone never converts a placed mine.

### Friendship rewards

Visit them after your friendship reaches each level to pick up the reward through dialogue.

- **Friendship 50:** gives you **1 Glow Ring**.
- **Friendship 75:** teaches the town to craft **Glow Rings** at a jewelry workplace with **Jewelry 5**. Choose **Browse all plans** to find the recipe.
- **Friendship 100:** gives you **1 Large Glow Ring**.
