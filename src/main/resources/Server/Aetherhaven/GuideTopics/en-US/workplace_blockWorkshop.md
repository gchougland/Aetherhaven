# Block Workshop

Apprentices make planks, bricks, stairs, slabs, roofs and other building blocks to order.

## Set it up

1. Recruit the **Builder**, finish their work building and unlock **Block Workshop** through their Apprentice workplace controls. You need a free workplace slot under that supervisor.
2. Use the **Building Staff**, choose **Create workplace**, and mark two opposite ground corners inside town. Choose **Block Workshop**, name the area and save. Include the whole **Builder's Workbench** in its marked area and height.
3. Employ a civilian Townsfolk with a nonzero **Building** skill. On the journal's **Work** page, enable **Block Workshop**; priority **1** is highest. Pay their wages and leave a clear walking route.

Select **Block Workshop**, not Builder's Yard, when saving the area. Open the bench's task page and choose recipes. Order a quantity, continuous work or a stock target, then provide ingredients in town storage.

## Work and output

Finished blocks go to **Crafted Goods** and can be collected on the task page or at the warehouse. The worker stays at the bench while crafting; they do not place these blocks into a construction project.

## If work is waiting

Enable the **Block Workshop** priority. Check the selected recipe's Building skill, bench tier and unlock requirements. The same bench opens a different page when it belongs to a Builder's Yard.

Keep the area nearby and loaded. Workers also take breaks for their needs. Open **Apprentice workplaces** for shared staff controls, **Work priorities and wages** for employment, and **Warehouse and deliveries** for supplies.
