# Floating gifts

Sometimes a wrapped gift drifts above town. Shoot it down before it floats away.

Gifts come in three colors. Each color holds different treasures inside a matching gift box.

White gifts often hold building blueprints, gold coins, and useful supplies.

Green gifts hold jewelry, gold coins, and useful supplies.

Red gifts hold furniture pieces, gold coins, and useful supplies.
