# Plot tokens

**Plot tokens** are items you hold in inventory to pick a building type on the **plot placement staff**, then stake the footprint in the world.

## Plot crafting bench

Craft plot tokens at the **plot crafting bench**. The bench shows a **3D preview** of the building before you commit materials. Craft the bench at the **town planning desk**, then place it like any workstation.

Most story quests still hand you the first token directly from the quest giver. After that, replacements and extra copies usually come from the bench.

## Plot blueprints

Some buildings are gated behind **plot blueprints** instead of dropping ready-made tokens from balloons.

1. **Use** the blueprint item to permanently unlock that plot at your plot crafting bench.
2. Open the bench and craft the plot token (materials plus gold, same as other tokens).
3. Equip the **plot placement staff** and stake the footprint.

**White floating gifts** often carry blueprints. Green gifts favor jewelry; red gifts favor furniture. Balloon gifts are rarer than before.

## Salvage

Extra plot tokens — including variant tokens from gifts — can be **salvaged** for gold coins at a workbench when you do not need another copy.

**Tips**

* Read **Player shop and shop spots** under Mechanics if a building includes merchant stalls.
* Florist, Pyrotechnic, and Crystal Keeper shop quests grant blueprints rather than direct tokens.
