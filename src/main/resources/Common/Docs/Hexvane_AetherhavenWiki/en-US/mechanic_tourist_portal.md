# Tourist portal

The **tourist portal** draws travelers from beyond your town borders. **Lyren the Elder** offers the build quest after the **town hall** is standing.

## Building the portal

1. Talk to Lyren and accept the tourist portal quest.
2. Take the tourist portal plot token from Lyren.
3. Stake and build the tourist portal prefab.
4. Find Lyren and close the quest in dialogue.

## Daily visits

Each morning, **tourists** can arrive through the portal. They walk around town, browse **shop spots** (including your player shop), and leave at **night**.

Tourists are hand-picked characters with personality traits that shape their dialogue. They are separate from **inn visitors** — see **Inn pool and visitors** under Mechanics for the inn's two-guest rotation.

## Inviting tourists to stay

If you want a tourist to become a permanent resident, offer them a **house**:

1. Build a house plot and assign them on the management block through the appropriate housing quest flow.
2. Once registered, they count toward town life like other residents.

Tourists who shop at player listings spend real gold from their pockets — a good reason to stock your shop spots with useful goods.

## Tips

* Finish **Your Own Shop** (from Vex) before expecting much tourist spending — they buy from player shop spots.
* The portal and inn serve different roles: the inn locks story **visitors** for quests; the portal brings repeat **tourists** each day.
